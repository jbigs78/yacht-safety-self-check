# Yacht Safety Equipment Self-Check

Online owner self-check form for Australian Sailing Special Regulations equipment audits (Category 4 & 7),
used by Port Macquarie Yacht Club equipment auditors.

Yacht owners open this form from the link in the email their auditor sends them. The yacht's details are carried
in the link itself (after the `#`, which is never sent to the web server). This page contains no yacht, owner or
audit data, and answers are kept only in the owner's own browser until they send them to their auditor by email.

This page is generated by the auditor's program (Settings → Download form page). To update it, replace `index.html`.
